# Code of Conduct

Ultimo adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone taking part in the project —
in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any other space the
project runs — and to people representing the project elsewhere.

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree about code, not about people.
- Welcome newcomers and questions at every level of experience.
- Give feedback that is specific and useful; accept it the same way.
- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualized language or
  imagery, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

The full text of the Covenant linked above is what applies; this summary
doesn't replace it.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that breaks this code, report it
privately to the maintainers by
[opening a private report](https://github.com/ultimo-rs/ultimo/security/advisories/new)
with a title starting "Code of conduct". Only maintainers can see it. You can
also use GitHub's **Report content** option on any comment, issue or pull
request.

Every report is reviewed and answered. Reporters' privacy is respected, and
maintainers will not take part in handling a report that involves them.

## Enforcement

Maintainers decide on a response in line with the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban,
depending on the severity and whether the behavior repeats.
